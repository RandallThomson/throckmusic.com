The Throckmorton Trio inhabits a strange, beautiful crossroads where Jazz Age parlor songs drift into British psychedelia. Crooner-composer Randall Throckmorton draws listeners toward the Victrola horn, conjuring Tin Pan Alley melodies, curious lyrics, and the occasional long-lost chord.

Randall is also known for work with the [Deadly Nightshade Family Singers](https://open.spotify.com/artist/0GPEJWNBUtfnkFdpk4EZCO/), [Dreamland Faces](https://dreamlandfaces.com/), The Selby Avenue Syncopators and Le Cirque Rouge Cabaret and Burlesque.

Multi-instrumentalists Christopher Thompson and Tom Wadzinski shape the trio's sound with their vocals, adventuresomeness, and deft solos, creating unexpected textures throughout a driving yet dreamlike repertoire.

Christopher Thompson currently plays bass and piano with [Stephanie Was & The Secret Izz](https://stephwasthesecretizz.bandcamp.com/album/heres-what-i-saw) and Le Cirque Rouge Cabaret.

Tom Wadzinski is very active in the Bluegrass and Oldtime community and a mandolinist and singer with [Blue Groove Bluegrass](https://www.bluegroovebluegrass.com).

The trio blends originals with a wide range of lesser performed covers, from classical art songs and Appalachian ballads to art-rock and torch songs, maintaining a distinctive intimacy centered around Randall's vintage crooning style.

Their original songs move between the jaunty and dark, dreamy and intimate, carried by a voice that is unmistakable and expressive.

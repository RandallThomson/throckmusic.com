Led by Randall Throckmorton’s distinctive high-voiced singing and original songs, the trio brings together multi-instrumentalists Tom Wadzinski and Christopher Thompson, whose mandolin, piano, and layered vocals shape an intimate, drumless acoustic setting.

{left}
Musically, their sound carries currents of Jazz Age parlor songs, art rock, Appalachian folk, classical art song and early psychedelia, drawn from the members' diverse musical backgrounds. Originals mingle with forgotten melodies and lesser-heard songs, drifting between the playful, dark, dreamy, and strange. Expect curious lyrics, unexpected turns, playful improvisation, and the occasional long-lost chord you didn’t realize you were missing—a performance that can feel like being drawn into the horn of a Victrola or the amber glow of a late-night radio dial.

{left}

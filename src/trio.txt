The Throckmorton Trio takes listeners on a musical journey where the distant past and the psychedelic ’60s meet. Drawing inspiration from Jazz Age standards, Tin Pan Alley, British psychedelia, and obscure early recordings, the trio uncovers forgotten melodies and gives them a fresh, sometimes dreamlike life.

Led by the distinctive, high-voiced singing of Randall Throckmorton expect curious lyrics, unexpected musical turns, playful improvisation, and the occasional long-lost chord.

Their music can feel simultaneously antique and otherworldly—like discovering a forgotten record in an attic and finding that it somehow still speaks to the present. Come ready for an eclectic, intimate performance that rewards curiosity and a willingness to follow the music wherever it wanders.

{left}
[Randall Throckmorton](about.html) is known for work with the [Deadly Nightshade Family Singers](https://open.spotify.com/artist/0GPEJWNBUtfnkFdpk4EZCO/), [Dreamland Faces](https://dreamlandfaces.com/), [The Selby Avenue Syncopators](https://youtu.be/fqJm-yxWeLs), and [Le Cirque Rouge Cabaret and Burlesque](https://www.instagram.com/lecirquerouge/).

Multi-instrumentalists Christopher Thompson and Tom Wadzinski round out the trio, shaping its sound with layered vocals and deft musicianship that lend unexpected textures to a repertoire equal parts driving and dreamlike. Christopher plays bass and piano with [Stephanie Was & The Secret Izz](https://stephwasthesecretizz.bandcamp.com/album/heres-what-i-saw) and [Le Cirque Rouge Cabaret and Burlesque](https://www.instagram.com/lecirquerouge/). Tom is deeply active in the Bluegrass and Oldtime community, and a mandolinist and singer with [Blue Groove Bluegrass](https://www.bluegroovebluegrass.com).

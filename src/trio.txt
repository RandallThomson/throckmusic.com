Led by Randall Throckmorton’s distinctive high-voiced singing and original songs, the trio brings together Tom Wadzinski’s deft mandolin and Christopher Thompson’s inventive piano in an intimate, drumless acoustic setting.

{left}
Musically, they inhabit a strange crossroads where Jazz Age parlor songs, Appalachian folk, classical art song, torch songs, and early psychedelia converge. Originals mingle with forgotten melodies and lesser-heard songs, drifting between the playful, dark, dreamy, and strange. Expect curious lyrics, unexpected turns, playful improvisation, and the occasional long-lost chord you didn’t realize you were missing—a performance that can feel like being drawn into the horn of a Victrola or the amber glow of a late-night radio dial.

{left}
Multi-instrumentalists Christopher Thompson and Tom Wadzinski round out the trio, shaping its sound with layered vocals and musicianship that lend unexpected textures to a repertoire equal parts driving and acrobatic. 

{left}
Christopher plays bass and piano with [Stephanie Was & The Secret Izz](https://stephwasthesecretizz.bandcamp.com/album/heres-what-i-saw) and [Le Cirque Rouge Cabaret and Burlesque](https://www.instagram.com/lecirquerouge/). 

{left}
Tom is deeply active in the Bluegrass and Oldtime community, and a mandolinist and singer with [Blue Groove Bluegrass](https://www.bluegroovebluegrass.com).

{left}
[Randall Throckmorton](about.html) is known for work with the [Deadly Nightshade Family Singers](https://open.spotify.com/artist/0GPEJWNBUtfnkFdpk4EZCO/), [Dreamland Faces](https://dreamlandfaces.com/), [The Selby Avenue Syncopators](https://youtu.be/fqJm-yxWeLs), and [Le Cirque Rouge Cabaret and Burlesque](https://www.instagram.com/lecirquerouge/).
